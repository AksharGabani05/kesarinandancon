FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? We have answers. Can't find what you're looking for? Contact us directly.

How long does it take to get my documents processed?
Most document services are processed within 1–3 working days. Tatkal and urgent services are available for faster processing. Same-day processing is available for certain services.
What documents do I need to carry when I visit?
You should carry your original Aadhaar card, PAN card (if applicable), 2–4 passport size photos, address proof, mobile number, and email ID. Specific services may require additional documents — call us to confirm.
Do you offer home delivery of completed documents?
Yes, for certain services we offer document delivery assistance. Please contact us at 7600617002 to discuss delivery options for your specific requirement.
What are your office working hours?
We are open Monday to Saturday from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM. On Sundays, we are open from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM. We are available on WhatsApp 24/7 for inquiries.
Is my personal data safe with Kesari Nandan Consultancy?
Absolutely. We take data privacy very seriously. All your documents and personal information are handled with strict confidentiality and are never shared with any third party without your consent.
Can I track the status of my application?
Yes! Once your application is submitted, we will provide you with a reference number. You can call us or WhatsApp us anytime to get the latest status update on your application.
